Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does a portable ice fan keep you cool?

Our handheld ice fans combine a high-speed brushless motor with a real ice-compress cooling plate. The fan pushes a strong, steady airflow while the metal cooling plate sits cold against your skin, so you feel an instant chill on your face, neck or wrists — far cooler than a standard mini fan.

How long does the battery last on one charge?

Battery life depends on the speed setting. On lower speeds our fans run for up to 6–8 hours, and on the highest setting around 2–3 hours. They recharge over USB-C, so you can top them up from a laptop, power bank or phone charger.

Is the IceWind Fan quiet enough to use at work?

Yes. The motor is designed for ultra-silent running, so it's comfortable to use at a desk, in the office, on public transport or while you sleep — without disturbing the people around you.

Can I use it hands-free, around my neck or on a desk?

Absolutely. The foldable models fold flat so the handle becomes a stand for hands-free desk use, and the head tilts so you can direct the airflow exactly where you want it. They're light enough to hold for long periods or pop in a bag for on-the-go cooling.

How fast is delivery and where do you ship from?

All orders are dispatched the next working day from our London warehouse with free tracked UK delivery, typically arriving within 1–3 days. You'll receive a tracking link so you can follow your parcel to your door.

What is your returns policy?

We offer easy 30-day returns. If you're not happy with your IceWind Fan, contact us at icewindfan@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a refund or replacement. See our Returns Policy page for full details.
